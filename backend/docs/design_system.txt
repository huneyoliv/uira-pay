# Donezo Design System Model (UIRÁ Payments - Dashboard Focus)

## 1. Philosophy
- **Organic & Friendly**: Highly rounded corners (`rounded-3xl`), soft shadows, and a nature-inspired palette.
- **Clean & Spacious**: Generous padding, white cards on light gray backgrounds.
- **Visual Hierarchy**: clear distinction between primary actions (Deep Green) and secondary elements.

## 2. Typography
- **Font Family**: Plus Jakarta Sans, Outfit, or Inter.
- **Headings**:
  - Bold, Dark (`text-slate-900`).
  - Large and friendly.
- **Body**:
  - `text-slate-500` for secondary text.
  - Medium weight for labels.

## 3. Color Palette
- **Primary**: Forest Green.
  - Main: `#105D37` (Deep Green) - Used for primary buttons, active states, key metric cards.
  - Light: `#E7F6F0` (Mint Green) - Used for backgrounds, badges.
- **Secondary**:
  - Dark: `#1E293B` (Slate 900) - Text.
  - Gray: `#F1F5F9` (Slate 100) - App Background.
- **Accents**:
  - Orange/Yellow for specific status indicators.
  - White for cards (`#FFFFFF`).

## 4. Layout & Shapes
- **Container**: Fluid dashboard layout with fixed sidebar.
- **Cards**:
  - `bg-white`.
  - `rounded-[32px]` (Very rounded).
  - `shadow-sm` or `shadow-[0_2px_20px_rgba(0,0,0,0.04)]`.
  - Padding: `p-6` or `p-8`.

## 5. Components

### Sidebar
- **Background**: White (`bg-white`) or very light gray.
- **Navigation**:
  - Inactive: `text-slate-400`.
  - Active: `text-[#105D37]` with a left border indicator or subtle background.
- **CTA**: "Download App" card at bottom with dark background.

### Header
- **Search**: Pill-shaped input (`rounded-full`), light gray background.
- **Actions**: Circular icon buttons.
- **Profile**: Avatar with name and email.

### Dashboard Widgets
- **Metrics Cards**:
  - Primary metric in large font.
  - Trend indicator in a circle arrow.
  - Some cards have full solid background (Primary Green), others are white.
- **Charts**:
  - Rounded bars.
  - Clean grid lines (or no grid lines).
- **Lists**:
  - Avatars for team members.
  - Status badges (pill shaped).

## 6. Implementation Guidelines (Tailwind CSS)
- **Radius**: Use `rounded-2xl` or `rounded-3xl` for cards. `rounded-full` for buttons/inputs.
- **Shadows**: `shadow-sm` for subtle depth.
- **Colors**:
  - Primary Button: `bg-[#105D37] hover:bg-[#0d4a2c] text-white`.
  - Background: `bg-[#F8FAFC]`.
